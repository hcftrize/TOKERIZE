T-RIZE & RIZE News — September 2026

October 1, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

We break down in this article both T-RIZE and RIZE developments that happened throughout September 2026 – a very productive and busy month to kick off this article series. Tokerize will break down the news monthly under a similar format for the months to come.

September — Week 1

August 25th: RIZE landed on Canton Network testnet natively under CIP-56 compatibility, powered by Chainlink CCIP – suggesting the token could arrive and be listed soon on Canton Network mainnet platforms.

September 1st: T-RIZE attended the RWA Summit in Brooklyn, a major RWA event for building business connections. Key participants noted included BlackRock, NYSE Group, DTCC, Digital Asset, Centrifuge, Securitize, S&P Dow Jones Indices, Chainlink, J.P. Morgan, Franklin Templeton, DRW Cumberland, CME Group, Fidelity Investments, GBBC, Moody’s, Deloitte, VanEck, Tokenized Assets Coalition, Nasdaq, Bank of America, Particula Ratings, and Grayscale.

September 3rd: T-RIZE announced joining Arc mainnet.

September — Week 2

September 10th: T-RIZE co-hosted a validator with Digital Asset on Canton Network mainnet. Digital Asset is the firm behind Canton, backed by industry leaders such as Goldman Sachs, DTCC, Broadridge, BNP Paribas, CME Group, Deutsche Börse Group, J.P. Morgan, and more.

September 10th: On Canton Network testnet, two new party IDs called “T-RIZE Registrar” and “T-RIZE Provider” were multi-hosted by T-RIZE, Digital Asset, and MPCH. MPCH is a cybersecurity firm and a Canton Network super validator, which recently and explicitly mentioned its interest in the T-RIZE Canton Intelligence Framework.

September 11th: The team added Aerodrome DEX liquidity to support RIZE’s transition toward a more organic trading environment, ahead of it becoming a Canton Network native token.

September 14-15th: T-RIZE attended the Fintech Canada 2026 event two days in a row, with a dedicated booth highlighting tokenization and federated learning.

September 14th: DTCC, Euroclear, ISDA, ERC3643, Digital Asset, Canton Network, Canton Foundation, and the Tokenized Assets Coalition were mentioned at the T-RIZE booth during the Fintech Canada forum, hinting at potential new partnerships. DTCC handles more than $4 quadrillion in volume yearly, while Euroclear handles about €1.4 quadrillion.

September — Week 3

September 15th: T-RIZE attended the Canton Summit in NYC, a private event gathering Canton leaders such as Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, DTCC, HSBC, Visa, Franklin Templeton, Kyriba, Broadridge, and others, discussing 24/7 markets, collateral, and moving assets across global markets on-chain.

September 16-17th: T-RIZE attended the “ALL IN” event in Montreal, one of the most important AI events in North America.

September — Week 4

September 22nd: T-RIZE announced the deployment of the RIZEMIND framework on Arc mainnet. Arc launched with more than 100 institutional and ecosystem builders, including a founding validator cohort of BlackRock, Visa, Mastercard, DTCC, SBI Group, ICE, and Standard Chartered. RIZEMIND brings federated intelligence to this emerging institutional and agentic ecosystem – open-source federated learning infrastructure that lets institutions train and improve models together while keeping sensitive data inside each organization.

September 23rd: T-RIZE led an event at ÉTS Montréal covering asset tokenization and the future of finance, where students were introduced to how markets, infrastructure, and financial instruments are being rebuilt on new technological foundations.

September 24th: T-RIZE announced it is now a member of the Canton Foundation’s Collateral Subcommittee, chaired by DTCC. Members help shape the standards and operating frameworks for collateral mobility across Canton Network. Madani Boukalba, Founder and CEO of T-RIZE, represents the company alongside institutions including DTCC, BNY, Euroclear Group, ISDA, and Broadridge.

September 25th: On Canton Network testnet, a “CarbonZero Registrar” party ID appeared, hosted by T-RIZE, Digital Asset, and MPCH. CarbonZero is the foundation behind the RIZE token.

September 28th: T-RIZE is attending SIBOS, the world’s premier annual conference, exhibition, and networking event for the global financial services industry, organized by SWIFT. Key participants include J.P. Morgan, Citi, HSBC, Deutsche Bank, Santander Corporate & Investment Banking, Barclays, Société Générale, Scotiabank, First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), Microsoft, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud, Mastercard, Visa, Accenture, EY, PwC, Deloitte, LSEG, and more.

September 28th: T-RIZE took the stage at Exploit Summit in Montreal, where Michael Duchesne, Head of Engineering, presented RIZEMIND and explored contribution scoring, distributed evaluation, privacy-preserving learning, and verifiable AI.

September 28th: The team released a new RIZE FAQ in the official Telegram channel, answering key questions around the RIZE token’s structure and utility, while Whitepaper V2 remains pending.

September 30th: T-RIZE announced that three team members will attend TOKEN2049 in Singapore on October 7th and 8th.

That closes out an eventful September for T-RIZE and RIZE – Tokerize will be back with the next monthly recap in October.
