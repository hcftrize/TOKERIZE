August 31, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of August 24–30, 2026, covering TECHNOLOGY, INSTITUTIONS, ECOSYSTEM, TOKENIZATION.

IntellectEU and Noves Integrate Private Data Layer Into CatalyX, Closing a Key Gap for Institutional Canton Deployments: IntellectEU has integrated the Noves Data Platform into CatalyX Blockchain Manager, bringing private financial and operational data infrastructure into a single production environment for more than 100 active Canton validators. Read more.

HANDL Opens Founders Circle While Work on 2.0 Continues: HANDL has created a private group for long-term holders, builders and power users who want to work more closely with the team. Read more.

Edel Markets Moves Toward Public Beta: Edel Finance is preparing to bring Edel Markets into public beta through Supanova, giving the new Canton venue access to an app that reports 80,000 users. Read more.

Digital Asset Details Canton's Post-Quantum Security Plans: Canton plans to introduce security methods designed to resist future quantum-computer attacks over the next six to twelve months. Partners are already testing an experimental digital-signature option. Read more.

Canton Development Fund Approves Up to 1.61 Million CC for Hacken and BootNode: Hacken and BootNode have received approval for Development Fund grants totaling up to 1.61 million CC. The funding supports security monitoring and tools for Canton developers, with payments tied to milestones. Read more.

Canton Foundation Publishes Development Fund Roadmap Through 2028: The Canton Foundation has outlined 28 areas for development funding, including simpler account hosting, better tools for builders and stronger security. The roadmap also sets growth targets for 2028. Read more.

OneSwap goes live inside Slay Money: Slay Money users can now swap Canton assets inside the app through its OneSwap integration, without opening another tab or reconnecting their wallet. Read more.

ZeroVaultID Activates Validator on Canton MainNet: ZeroVaultID has activated its Canton MainNet validator. It is now seeking a partner for the first MainNet deployment of its privacy-focused compliance tools. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Vanguard and Wellington Test Tokenized Collateral on Canton: Vanguard and Wellington Management have completed their first tokenized collateral test trades on Canton. Nasdaq Calypso managed the process around each trade, while the collateral moved and settled on Canton. Read more.

World Liberty Financial Launches USD1 Directly on Canton Network: World Liberty Financial’s USD1 stablecoin is now issued directly on Canton Network. Institutions can configure it as the dollar payment for tokenized assets, allowing the asset and payment to settle together. Read more.

Circle's USYC seeks Canton Featured App Status: Circle's USYC has applied for Featured App status on Canton, with Digital Asset as sponsor. The tokenized money-market fund is already live on the network. Read more.

Virtu Financial, M1X Global, and Tradeweb Complete First Fully On-Chain Repo Using Sovereign Digital Bond on Canton Network: Virtu Financial, M1X Global, and Tradeweb have completed the first fully on-chain repo using a natively issued sovereign digital bond as collateral on Canton Network, settling the full cycle atomically in under 10 minutes without prime broker intermediation. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

Cenote Moves Under Kintsu as Canton Credit Platform Becomes Kintsu Capital Markets: Kintsu is bringing Cenote’s Canton-focused credit work into its wider platform. The Cenote brand will retire, while the product under development continues as Kintsu Capital Markets. Read more.

Console Wallet Launches First Visa Card on Canton Network, Allowing Direct Spending of Canton Coin: Console Wallet has launched the first Visa card on Canton Network following successful initial testing, allowing users to spend Canton Coin directly at any card-accepting merchant through Apple Pay or Google Pay. Read more.

Cantex Launches Cantex Connect, Bringing Wallet-Agnostic Trading to Its Exchange: Cantex has introduced Cantex Connect, allowing any wallet in the Canton ecosystem that supports a transfer memo to trade on Cantex through a native token transfer, with no permissioned integration, browser extension, or SDK required. Read more.

Temple Approved for DvP Treatment on Canton: Temple has received approval for DvP treatment on Canton. The decision covers qualifying trades where the asset and payment settle together in a single transaction. Read more.

Canborsa Enables Canton Coin as Collateral, Deepening Its Canton Network Integration: Canborsa now supports Canton Coin as collateral for perpetual trading, allowing users to trade CC/USDCx with up to 5x leverage. Hecto Reveals Its Next Three Products on Canton: Hecto has set out its next three product routes, with Predictions V2, PMX and Vaults built for different parts of the private-market audience. Read more.

Alpend Integrates Temple for Collateral Management on Canton: Alpend has integrated Temple's trading system into its early-access lending market. The connection will support collateral sales during liquidations, with the aim of improving recoveries. Read more.

Sixteen Validator Applications Open for Canton Review: Sixteen organizations have applied to operate Canton validators, with community review open until September 2. Their plans include wallets, tokenized assets and services for financial institutions. Read more.

mperps Introduces Canton Perpetual Trading With Aster: mperps has introduced perpetual trading with Aster, with account balances recorded on Canton. Access requires an invitation code, while Hyperliquid and Lighter are planned additions. Read more.

HANDL adds EDEL transfers between Base and Canton: HANDL users can now move EDEL between Base and Canton inside the app. The bridge expands the existing options to receive, hold and swap Edel's token on Canton. Read more.

Canton Foundation Development Fund adopts rules for AI assisted proposals: A new policy sets out how contributors can use AI for Development Fund work, including funding proposals, code and milestone submissions. Read more.

Cantex Trading Volume Passes $200 Million: Cantex has reported more than $200 million in cumulative trading volume, just over eight days after announcing its $150 million milestone. The update follows the launch of Cantex Connect, which lets compatible Canton wallets trade without opening the platform’s interface. Read more.

OKJ Launches Canton Coin Quiz With 70,000 CC in Rewards: OKJ has opened a ten-question Canton Coin quiz running until September 18. Participants who answer at least seven questions correctly can share 60,000 CC, with another 10,000 CC reserved for ten selected qualifiers. Read more.
