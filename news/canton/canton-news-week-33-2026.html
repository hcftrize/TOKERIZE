CANTON NEWS — WEEK 33, 2026

August 17, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of August 10–16, 2026, covering ANALYSIS, TECHNOLOGY, INSTITUTIONS, TOKENIZATION, ECOSYSTEM.

ANALYSIS

Canton Network Leads All Blockchains in 30-Day Fee Generation at $53.35 Million: Canton Network has generated $53.35 million in fees over the past 30 days, ranking first among all blockchains globally ahead of Hyperliquid and Tron, according to DeFiLlama data. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

Rocky Exchange Wins HackCanton Season 2 as Umbra and Tirai Complete the Podium: More than 360 builders from over 60 countries took part in HackCanton Season 2, with Rocky Exchange finishing first ahead of Umbra and Tirai. Read more.

OpenZeppelin Opens Its Canton Development Stack to Public Review: OpenZeppelin has made early Daml components and reference architectures for Canton public, including CIP-112 token and settlement tooling. The materials invite developer testing, feedback and further implementation work. Read more.

Digital Asset Releases Canton 2.10.6 to Improve Post-Migration Reconnects: Canton 2.10.6 is a maintenance release that targets reconnect performance after hard domain migrations for PostgreSQL-based deployments with large active contract sets. It also updates dependencies to resolve Netty security issues. Read more.

CertiK Maps the Full Security Model Behind Canton Assets: CertiK’s new guide explains how CIP-56 gives Canton assets a common interface, while implementation, client transaction construction and interpretation, and operational permissions collectively shape their security. The piece focuses on the security boundaries that institutions and developers should assess. Read more.

Nuxaris: Private cross-chain bridge into Canton Network.: Nuxaris is a Canton-native, non-custodial bridge that enables private, bidirectional transfers between Canton Coin and supported networks including Solana, Ethereum and Base. It combines multi-wallet support with a simple route into and out of the Canton ecosystem. Read more.

Digital Asset Releases Canton 3.5.13 With CantonBFT and Traffic Enforcement Improvements: Digital Asset has released Canton 3.5.13, a maintenance update focused on CantonBFT and traffic enforcement. It refines how nodes retrieve missing batches during connectivity issues and adds new optional controls for participant operators. Read more.

Splice 0.7.2 Adds Session Key Support and Updates SV Voting: Splice 0.7.2 moves to Canton 3.5.13, adds official session signing key support for KMS enabled validator and Super Validator participants, and updates how SV votes are handled while requests remain open. Read more.

HackCanton Season 3 Opens Registration for Five-Week Online Builder Competition: HackCanton Season 3 begins September 10 and runs online for five weeks, with workshops, mentoring and a live Grand Final. Registration is open to solo builders and teams, with up to $50,000 in cash and credits listed for the event. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Broadridge's Blockchain Repo Platform Processes $8 Trillion in July, Deepening Ties to Canton Network: Broadridge Financial Solutions processed $8 trillion in repo transactions through its Distributed Ledger Repo platform in July, as the company continues to deepen its role in the Canton Network ecosystem, alongside a cross-border intraday repo transaction involving DTCC, Euroclear, Tradeweb, Citadel Securities, and Societe Generale settled on Canton. Read more.

Digital Asset CEO Says It Is Time for a Fuller Korea Entry: In an original BusinessPost interview, Yuval Rooz said he believes it is an appropriate time for Digital Asset to enter Korea in earnest, with institutional Canton use a priority. He cited insurance as a possible use case and said further announcements could follow in the coming weeks. Read more.

MUFG, Digital Asset and Progmat Launch Canton JGB Repo Pilot: MUFG and three group companies have joined Digital Asset and Progmat to launch a Canton proof of concept for Japanese government-bond repo transactions. It will explore simultaneous settlement of JGBs and digital money, together with a more automated repo lifecycle. Read more.

Canton Strategic Reports $1.5 Million in Second-Quarter Revenue: Canton Strategic Holdings reported $1.5 million in second-quarter revenue, including $191,226 from network validation and $1.3 million in other revenue, primarily from locking-as-a-service agreements. Read more.

Franklin Templeton Clears First Canton Treasury-Management Milestone: Canton’s Tokenomics Working Group has approved Franklin Templeton’s first CIP-0118 milestone as complete and approved it for the release of one unit of Super Validator weight. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

Ember Treasury Is Live on Canton, With Trading on Cantex: Ember Protocol has introduced Ember Treasury (USTY), which it describes as tokenized U.S. Treasury bills for onchain markets. Ember says USTY is live on Canton and available for trading on Cantex. Read more.

Cancore’s USDCx–USDC Route Goes Live Between Canton and Ethereum: Cancore has made a route for USDCx on Canton and USDC on Ethereum available through an atomic settlement flow that it says keeps assets with counterparties until settlement completes. Read more.

Cantor8 Announces Taran Route to Gold, Silver and T-Bills on Canton: Cantor8 has announced that merchants using Taran App can exchange tokenized mobile money for gold, silver and Treasury-bill products on Canton through C8 Wallet. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

OneSwap Launches First 'Pump.fun-Style' Token Launchpad on Canton Network: OneSwap has announced the first Pump.fun-like token launchpad built on Canton Network, letting users create, add liquidity to, and trade tokens in minutes, with the platform going live soon. Read more.

Temple Goes Live on Token Terminal as Canton Network's Top Revenue Application: Temple Digital Group has listed on Token Terminal, making Canton Network's highest revenue-generating application publicly trackable on a major analytics platform for the first time. Read more.

Console Wallet Integrates LetsExchange API to Expand Canton Coin Swaps: Console Wallet has integrated the LetsExchange API, allowing Canton Coin holders to swap directly into stablecoins and EVM assets and fund their wallets from external networks, all within a single interface. Read more.
