CANTON NEWS — WEEK 20, 2026

May 18, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of May 11–17, 2026.

ECOSYSTEM

Privket Launches Canton Network's First NFT Collection: Privket’s Canton Penguin collection sold out on Canton Network, marking the chain’s first NFT drop and an early test of collectible activity on the network. Read more.

Cantor8 Plans Canton-Powered Mobile Money Pilot in Africa With Yiksi Limited: Cantor8 has secured exclusive MOUs with Yiksi Limited to explore bringing M-PESA and EVC Plus onto blockchain rails through Canton Network. Read more.

Rho Labs Announces Rho Relay, Bringing Private Cross-Chain Settlement to the Canton Network: Rho Labs has launched Rho Relay, giving EVM users a private, non-custodial route to access and trade Canton Coin through Canton-native cross-chain settlement. Read more.

Temple Crosses $100 Million in Trading Volume on Canton Network: Temple, the first native trading platform built on Canton Network, has surpassed $100 million in cumulative trading volume, marking a significant milestone for institutional on-chain trading within the Canton ecosystem. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

T-RIZE Launches $50M Private Credit Programme on Canton Network: T-RIZE Group has announced the Kairos Digital Loan Notes programme, opening with a $50 million tranche for eligible institutional and qualified investors. Built on Canton Network, the programme brings UK litigation-finance receivables into a governed digital issuance framework with controls for eligibility, transfers, compliance and lifecycle administration. Read more.

BlockBooster Joins Canton Foundation With Focus on Tokenized Credit: BlockBooster has joined the Canton Foundation, where it plans to contribute to governance, committee work, and ecosystem development focused on tokenized credit, fund tokenization, and real-world asset management standards. Read more.

Societe Generale to Scale Tokenized Finance on Canton: Societe Generale is expanding its work on Canton Network, with plans for tokenized collateral, on-chain financing, repo activity, and the deployment of SG-FORGE’s USD and EUR CoinVertible stablecoins. Read more.

Canton Strategic Deepens Canton Network Bet After First-Quarter Buildout: Canton Strategic ended the first quarter with 3.68 billion Canton Coin, $41.5 million in cash, Super Validator approval, and a strengthened capital base as it builds its public-market strategy around Canton Network infrastructure. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

Zenith surpasses 500,000+ EVM transactions in internal test environment: Zenith’s internal test environment has processed more than 500,000 EVM transactions through Canton Network, with a 100% success rate and 1.5-second average latency on an unoptimized stack. The update gives an early look at Zenith’s native EVM architecture for Canton ahead of public testnet access. Read more.

IntellectEU Launches Canton Onboarding Package for DTCC Tokenization Participants: IntellectEU has launched a Canton onboarding package for institutions in the DTCC Industry Working Group, covering node operations, wallet and custody integration, custom development, tokenomics advisory, and legacy system integration. Read more.
