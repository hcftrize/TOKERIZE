CANTON NEWS — WEEK 28, 2026

July 13, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of July 06–12, 2026, covering INSTITUTIONS, TECHNOLOGY, MARKETS.

INSTITUTIONS

Seven Days to Go: DTCC's Tokenization Service Prepares for Live Trading on Canton Network: DTCC will launch its tokenization service on the Canton Network on July 13, enabling the first live blockchain trading of DTC-custodied U.S. Treasury securities. The rollout marks a major step in bringing traditional financial assets on-chain, with a full launch planned for October 2026. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

NETSTARS and Canton Foundation Sign MOU to Explore Web3 Payments: NETSTARS and Canton Foundation have signed an MOU to explore Web3-based payment solutions, including stablecoin payments, with privacy, interoperability and compliance as core priorities. Read more.

Turnkey Brings Secure Key Management and Signing Infrastructure to Canton Network: Turnkey now supports Canton Network, providing developers with enclave-backed key management, Party registration, and transaction signing through a single API, removing the need to build custom signing infrastructure for Canton applications. Read more.

x402 Payments Go Live on Canton Network: FTP Tech has brought x402 payments to Canton Network, enabling APIs to charge per request and settle payments on-ledger in Canton Coin. Read more.

Digital Asset Co-Founder Outlines Plan to Extend CC Burning to Every Canton Synchronizer: Digital Asset co-founder Shaul Kfir has outlined plans to extend CC burning and validator rewards to all Canton synchronizers, with a CIP draft due in September and rollout targeted for the first half of 2027. Read more.

MARKETS

OKJ to Add Canton Coin (CC) on July 15: OKJ will begin supporting Canton Coin (CC) on July 15, 2026. Exchange trading, brokerage purchases, recurring purchases, deposits and withdrawals will be available. Read more.
