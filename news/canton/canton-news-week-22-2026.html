CANTON NEWS — WEEK 22, 2026

June 01, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of May 25–31, 2026, covering ECOSYSTEM, MARKETS, INSTITUTIONS.

ECOSYSTEM

OneSwap Launches as Canton Network's First Permissionless DEX: OneSwap, built by Draper Associates and Coinbase Ventures-backed Sats Terminal, has launched as a permissionless AMM on the Canton Network, opening CC, USDCx, and tokenized Bitcoin trading to retail users on institutional settlement infrastructure. Read more.

Send Opens Base-to-Canton Bridge for SEND, USDC, cbBTC and frxUSD: Selected Base assets can now bridge directly into Send App on Canton, giving users a path to bring SEND, USDC, cbBTC and frxUSD into Send Canton Wallet as wrapped .B assets. Read more.

How $CC Holders Can Put Their Tokens to Work Across DeFi: Canton DeFi is starting to give CC holders more ways to use their capital. From lending and borrowing to staking, liquidity pools, vaults, trading and bridges, there are now several live routes for putting CC and related assets to work. Read more.

Edel Finance Plans Canton-Based Perpetual Futures Exchange: Edel Finance plans to launch Edel Markets in Q3 2026, bringing on-chain perpetual futures for equities and commodities to Canton Network. Read more.

Canborsa Opens Beta for RWA Perpetuals on Canton Network: Canborsa has launched the beta of its Canton Network-based trading platform, offering a self-custodial wallet, onchain swaps, and perpetual trading for crypto and tokenized real-world assets. Read more.

MARKETS

Gate Enables USDCx Deposits and Withdrawals on Canton Network, Adds $150,000 CC Incentive Campaign: Gate has enabled USDCx deposits and withdrawals on Canton Network and launched a $150,000 CC incentive campaign spanning spot trading, deposit challenges, and fixed-term savings products. Read more.

Canton Network Tops Global Protocol Rankings by Revenue With $63.9 Million in 30 Days: Canton Network has topped DeFiLlama's global protocol rankings by 30-day revenue, generating $63.9 million as its 100% fee burn model returns the entirety of network activity to participants. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Bitwise Launches Canton ETP on Deutsche Börse Xetra: Bitwise has launched the Bitwise Canton ETP on Deutsche Börse Xetra, giving investors exchange-traded exposure to CC, the native token of Canton Network. The product is issued by Bitwise Europe GmbH in Germany and is fully backed by CC tokens held in cold storage. Read more.
