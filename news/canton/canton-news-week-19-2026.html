CANTON NEWS — WEEK 19, 2026

May 11, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of May 04–10, 2026, covering TECHNOLOGY, ANALYSIS, INSTITUTIONS, TOKENIZATION, ECOSYSTEM, MARKETS.

TECHNOLOGY

Canton Ends Passive Validator Rewards as Incentives Shift Toward Usage: Canton Network has ended liveness rewards for validators, moving its incentive model away from passive node operation and toward usage, application demand, and long-term alignment. Read more.

ANALYSIS

DeFi’s Security Reckoning Makes Canton Network’s Guardrails Harder to Dismiss: Digital Asset CEO Yuval Rooz says institutions need ways to keep bad actors away from the assets and systems they operate on-chain. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Northern Trust and LSEG Among 22 Applicants to Operate Canton Validators: Canton Network’s latest validator operator batch includes Northern Trust, LSEG and a wider set of infrastructure and application-focused firms. The list shows validator interest extending from major financial institutions to smaller operators ahead of a May 6 approval deadline. Read more.

Kresus and Canton Network Move to Bridge the Institutional Blockchain Execution Gap: Kresus and Canton Network have partnered to help financial institutions take blockchain projects from development into live deployment — starting with a tokenized asset platform for South Korea's Hanwha Investment & Securities. Read more.

AMINA Becomes First Bank to Support Canton Coin Trading and Custody: AMINA Bank has become the first regulated banking institution to offer custody and trading for Canton Coin, giving institutional clients and network participants a supervised entry point into Canton Network's growing capital markets infrastructure. Read more.

Canton Foundation’s Viv Diwakar Joins Bitcoin Seoul 2026 Speaker Lineup: Canton Foundation Head Viv Diwakar is listed among the speakers for Bitcoin Seoul 2026, which will take place June 4–5 at FKI Tower in Seoul. The event’s agenda is expected to focus on on-chain finance, real-world assets, stablecoins and AI, giving Canton a relevant stage in one of Asia’s major blockchain gatherings. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

DTCC Sets July Target for Initial Tokenized Securities Trades: DTCC plans to facilitate initial, limited production trades through DTC’s tokenization service in July 2026, with a broader launch planned for October. Digital Asset, identified by DTCC as creator of Canton Network, is among more than 50 firms in the industry working group. The update follows DTCC’s separate partnership with Digital Asset and Canton Network to tokenize a subset of DTC-custodied U.S. Treasuries on Canton. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

CIP-0105 Sets Locking Framework for Canton Super Validators: CIP-0105 introduces a voluntary locking framework for Canton Super Validators. The approved proposal links forward SV Weight to actively locked lifetime-earned Canton Coin rewards and sets rules for tiered weighting, unlock vesting, custody and enforcement. Read more.

Why Now Is the Time to Build on Canton Network: Canton is moving away from passive validator rewards and toward application activity, shared tooling, and ecosystem development. For eligible Featured Apps, rewards are now tied more directly to usage, while grants remain selective. The direction is clear: Canton’s next phase depends on builders turning its infrastructure into useful financial applications. Read more.

Temple Listed on CoinMarketCap as Top Global Exchange for $CC: Temple is now listed on CoinMarketCap, giving the Canton-native trading platform a clearer public market-data profile. The listing makes Temple easier for traders to find, compare and track, while showing the platform as the top global exchange for $CC by 24-hour notional volume during the cited window. Read more.

Canton Network Welcomes 33 New Validator Operators to Its Growing Infrastructure: Canton Network has welcomed 33 new validator operators, broadening the infrastructure base that supports the network's security and institutional-grade operations. Read more.

MARKETS

21Shares Launches First U.S. ETF Offering Exposure to Canton Coin on Nasdaq: 21Shares has listed what it calls the first U.S. ETF offering exposure to Canton Coin on Nasdaq, with Goldman Sachs, Microsoft, and Deutsche Bank among the institutions participating in the network — though not endorsing the fund. Read more.

Kaiko Indices Named Benchmark Provider for 21Shares Canton ETF: Kaiko Indices will provide the benchmark behind TCAN, the 21Shares Canton Network ETF listed on Nasdaq. The role adds an independent reference-rate layer to the fund and extends Kaiko’s existing work around $CC market data. Read more.

ACME Brings Lending to Canton as the Network Moves Beyond Settlement: ACME Lend is bringing lending and borrowing markets to Canton Network, testing whether the network’s privacy-focused infrastructure can support a deeper onchain credit layer. The launch adds another core financial primitive to a network that is still building out its application depth. Read more.

Digital Asset Targets $2 Billion Valuation as a16z Crypto Moves to Lead $300 Million Round: Digital Asset is seeking roughly $300 million in new funding at a $2 billion valuation, with a16z crypto expected to lead, as institutional demand for regulated blockchain infrastructure continues to draw significant capital. Read more.
