CANTON NEWS — WEEK 41, 2026

October 12, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of October 05–11, 2026.

TECHNOLOGY

Canton Venues brings seven trading venues into one dashboard: Canton Venues is a free dashboard for comparing token prices, liquidity and estimated trade costs across seven Canton venues. Its public data feed also supports developers and compatible AI tools. Read more.

DIA seeks funding for multi-source price checks on Canton: DIA is seeking up to 8.4 million CC to build an open-source tool that checks data from several providers before Canton applications use it. Funding remains under review. Read more.

Canton outlines its October 10 MainNet upgrade timetable: Canton’s planned move to CantonBFT includes a 24-hour freeze on network-configuration changes, followed by a brief transaction pause on October 10. Ordinary transactions are expected to continue during the freeze, while the on-chain scheduling vote remains open. Read more.

Canton advances Splice upgrades across MainNet and TestNet: Splice 0.8.3 is ready for MainNet deployment, while 0.8.4 has cleared the same stage on TestNet. Each October 5 notice confirms that two-thirds of Super Validator nodes had upgraded, including the Global Synchronizer Foundation’s node. Read more.

Canton draft proposes self-service validator onboarding: A new Canton governance draft would replace manual validator onboarding and IP whitelists with automatic access after a traffic purchase. It also sets security checks and staged testing requirements, but the proposal remains an unapproved draft. Read more.

Canton’s developer update adds dAppBooster demos and setup guides: BootNode’s dAppBooster write-up adds live examples and setup guides to Canton’s recent developer-tooling roundup. The open-source starter combines a local Canton environment with wallet connections and reusable interface components, giving builders a working example to adapt. Read more.

Logical Contracts seeks funding to help Ethereum apps move to Canton: Logical Contracts has requested 1.3 million CC for a tool that would convert Ethereum smart contracts into native Canton code and test whether they still behave as intended. The six-month proposal has not been approved. Read more.

IntellectEU releases AI test benchmark for Canton developers: IntellectEU has published a benchmark that measures how well AI agents write tests for Daml code. The public set contains 34 tasks and 159 deliberately introduced faults, with code and baseline results available for developers to examine. Read more.

Splice 0.9.0 reaches Canton’s DevNet: Splice 0.9.0 is available for Canton’s development network, with two-thirds of its Super Validator nodes already upgraded. The release updates wallet deployment, reward collection and services used by applications. Read more.

Canton proposal would extend CC burn across MainNet: A new draft would replace license fees for dedicated Canton synchronizers with CC burn and extend rewards to their apps and validators. It also proposes volume discounts, with detailed pricing to follow in a separate proposal. Read more.

PixelPlex proposes a simpler way to add Canton tokens to apps: PixelPlex’s Open Token Standard draft would give developers a consistent way to find the software and information needed to support Canton tokens. Issuers could opt in without changing existing transfer and settlement processes. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

Meriq seeks Featured App status for its Canton swap venue: Meriq has applied for Featured App status for its Canton asset-swap venue. The platform is operating in a controlled MainNet launch, with an independent audit still planned before larger production limits. Read more.

Cantor8 names winners of its August Canton hackathon: Team Mandate took first place in Cantor8's August hackathon, followed by Lattice Scan, Vorem and DamnLE. The October 5 results highlight controlled AI-agent spending, private indexing and security, with further details about the Cantor8 Accelerator still to come. Read more.

Kora tops $7 million in 24-hour trading volume: Kora's October 5 update puts its 24-hour trading volume above $7 million. The dated platform snapshot follows September listings for Canton Coin, gold and silver against USDCx, and measures completed trading rather than assets held on the exchange. Read more.

Modulo adds cSOL buying, selling and holding: cSOL is available through Modulo, with users able to buy, sell and hold it from their wallets. The October 5 announcement adds access through another platform, following cSOL's September listing on Silvana, rather than marking the token's first appearance on Canton. Read more.

Tradecraft passes $3.5 million in total value locked on Canton: Tradecraft's October 5 update puts total value locked on Canton above $3.5 million, following its September milestone of more than $3 million. TVL measures assets held in the platform's pools, not trading volume or revenue, and the figure is a platform-provided snapshot. Read more.

Canton Development Fund pays 5.08 million CC across four milestones: The Canton Development Fund has paid 5,081,500 CC across four milestones for Splice Portfolio, traffic accounting, BitSafe maintenance and Daml 3 training. The receipts confirm payment for defined work, not completion of the wider grant programs. Read more.

Zenith warns of fake token listings and scam testnet links: Zenith has warned users about fake links, token listings and faucets appearing after its testnet launch. ZTH is a test-only token with no monetary value, and no official Zenith token is available for trading. Read more.

Nocturnal Wallet enters Canton Featured App vote: Nocturnal Wallet has moved from committee approval to a MainNet vote for Featured App status. Voting closes on October 13, with October 14 set as the target effective date. Read more.

Cancore’s Genesis Points program passes 50,000 users: Cancore’s latest update puts participation in its Genesis Points program above 50,000 users. Swaps earn points, and no snapshot had been taken when the October 6 update was published. Read more.

Canborsa launches Savings Season 1 for stablecoin deposits: Canborsa has launched Savings Season 1 for USDT, USDC and USDCx deposits. The pool has a $300,000 capacity and a $25,000 limit per wallet, with daily payouts and a one-month boost taking the advertised rate to as much as 20% APR. Read more.

Canton awards MainNet allocations to 43 named organisations: Canton’s latest MainNet allocation notice names 43 distinct organisations, including Kaleido, OmniStake and MZF Protocol. The awards give recipients an allocation for a validator node; they do not confirm that the nodes are already running. Read more.

Unhedged launches October XP rewards with up to $3,000 per user: Unhedged’s new XP program awards points for trading fees and pays rewards as users reach higher levels. Its October season has 10 levels, starting with a $10 reward and offering up to $3,000 in total. Read more.

Fairmint proposes a common NFT format for Canton: Fairmint has proposed a shared format for unique assets on Canton, covering everything from tickets and memberships to regulated certificates. Equity certificates are its first planned production use. Read more.

Canton proposal adds a way to transfer locked CC: An update to CIP-0127 proposes a way to change the owner of locked Canton Coin while keeping the lock in place. It would let participants move to a new custodian without first unlocking or replacing the funds. Read more.

ForecastDesk seeks Canton Featured App status for prediction markets: ForecastDesk has applied for Featured App status following its September MainNet launch. The prediction platform lets users trade event outcomes from their own Canton wallets, with payments and positions settled together. Read more.

SoFi adds Canton Coin to its crypto app: SoFi customers can now buy, sell and hold Canton Coin in the app. CC is one of 11 recent additions that take SoFi Crypto’s supported range to 40 digital assets. Read more.

Nuxaris launches BNB bridging and cBNB swaps on Canton: Nuxaris has added BNB Chain support and ten new bridge routes. Users can bridge BNB into cBNB, swap it for Canton Coin and hold or send it through the platform’s wallet. Read more.

Canton approves 18 more validator operators: Canton’s October 7 batch approves 18 operators, including elebank limited and Enflux, with applicants planning tokenized deposits, trading and payment tools. Read more.

Brickken seeks Canton funding for a regulated-token standard: Brickken is seeking 5.85 million CC for a 15-month project to develop shared compliance controls for tokenized assets on Canton, with funding for an implementation, audit and developer tools. Read more.

Cantex adds Edel tokens linked to Nvidia and SpaceX: NVDAe and SPCXe are now trading on Cantex, expanding Edel’s Canton offering beyond its existing index-linked tokens. Access to the exchange remains invitation-only. Read more.

Kintsu opens its private beta on Canton: Kintsu has opened its private beta to early borrowers, lenders and vault managers. The project is developing lending markets, managed vaults and staking for Canton, with its current beta environment running on testnet. Read more.

BitSafe seeks Canton Featured App status for HYPE-backed CHYPE: BitSafe is seeking Featured App status for CHYPE, a planned HYPE-backed Canton asset, with Temple its first intended integration. Read more.

Maha Wallet partners with Cashen for a 5 million CC lock: Maha Wallet is partnering with Cashen to meet Canton’s Featured App locking requirement with 5 million CC. The arrangement addresses the capital requirement, which sits alongside a separate approval process. Read more.

BootNode seeks funding for a shared Canton app-testing toolkit: BootNode has proposed canton-testkit, an open-source library to help Canton teams test transactions from setup to ledger confirmation. It is requesting 702,000 CC, with payment tied to a working release and adoption in existing test suites. Read more.

CCTools expands portfolio tracking with NFTs and DeFi positions: CCTools has added value history, recent activity, supported NFTs and DeFi positions to its cross-network portfolio tracker. Read more.

Moonsong Labs invites institutions to help shape Canton vault platform: Moonsong Labs is recruiting more design partners for VaultKit, a toolkit for running investment products on Canton. Institutions keep the software in their own environment and help shape its development. Read more.

Digital Asset proposes a voting app for Canton Super Validator delegates: Digital Asset is seeking 1 million CC to let Super Validator operators authorise other parties to vote on their behalf. The proposed app would separate voting responsibilities from node operations while keeping one vote per Super Validator node. Read more.

Unhedged adds trading through AI assistants: Unhedged now connects with AI assistants for market research and trading. Users choose separate permissions for account access, trades and withdrawals. Read more.

CatalyX updates its Canton-focused Tokenization Explorer: CatalyX has updated its Tokenization Explorer with shareable analyses, fit scores and suggested next steps. The tool helps businesses assess potential uses for tokenization before committing to a build. Read more.

HestiaX operates a Canton MainNet validator: HestiaX runs a validator on Canton MainNet alongside its work on renewable-energy finance software. Its platform is designed to prepare project data and contracted revenues for institutional financing. Read more.

Nuxaris locks 25 million CC as cBNB seeks Featured App status: Nuxaris has locked 25 million Canton Coin through Cashen for cBNB. Its separate Featured App application focuses on bringing the BNB-backed asset to more Canton trading venues. Read more.

Canton Development Fund approves 1.2 million CC for BitDynamics: BitDynamics has secured funding approval for improvements to Splice, covering grant controls, transaction-cost monitoring and tools for network operators. The work is split into two milestones with a six-month target. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Utila joins DTCC's tokenization working group: Utila has joined DTCC's industry working group on institutional tokenization. It brings experience in managing Canton assets, approving transfers and connecting blockchain activity with the systems financial institutions already use. Read more.

Marex’s Canton Super Validator arrangement takes effect on MainNet: Canton has implemented the hosted reward-weight arrangement for Marex’s approved Super Validator program. Rewards remain tied to verified milestones covering institutional activity on the network. Read more.
