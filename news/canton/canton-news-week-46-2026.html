November 16, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Roundup: Send returns, Cancore adds Base and Supanova launches Thesis: Send restores its Canton Wallet, Cancore announces a seventh network connection, and new developer tools and funding decisions move forward. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

AnChain.AI seeks 1.6 million CC for Canton fraud-investigation tools: The proposed toolkit would turn authorised transaction records into evidence for human review, with most funding tied to live adoption. Read more.

The Case for One Network, One Economy (CIP-0129): CIP-0129 proposes bringing Canton’s Global and dedicated synchronizers under one economic framework through universal CC burn and shared reward eligibility. Here’s how its pricing model, payment mechanism and phased implementation could reshape Canton’s network economy. Read more.

Rome introduces Ostia, a ZK EVM on Canton DevNet: Developers can use Solidity tools and test transfers with Canton. Ostia checks blocks with cryptographic proofs before their Canton settlement. Read more.

Fairmint wins approval for a 3.5 million CC Privy toolkit on Canton: The approved plan would help apps use Privy-managed keys for Canton transactions. More than half the funding depends on independent adoption and maintenance. Read more.

Canton draft would let Super Validators delegate their governance votes: A proposed voting app would separate voting access from node operations while keeping one vote per Super Validator node. Read more.

Noves seeks 5.25 million CC to turn Canton activity into accounting records: The proposed open-source subledger would help finance teams reconcile balances, close their books and give auditors evidence behind the entries. Read more.

Fairmint and Blockworks’ 2.4 million CC asset-data proposal passes vote: The shared project would give Canton applications a common way to publish asset metrics. Funding is tied to two delivered feeds and verified adoption by Blockworks. Read more.

OpenZeppelin’s Canton library and four app designs pass development-fund review: The contracts library is available, while exchange, lending, payment and auction designs move toward implementation. Security audits are planned for the next phase. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

OpenAssets partners with Digital Asset to bring tokenization tools to Canton: The planned integration combines OpenAssets’ market platform with DA Registry, covering asset issuance, transfer controls and administration. Read more.

Cashen unveils cashBTC, a Bitcoin token for Canton planned for Q1 2027: The proposed asset combines 1:1 BTC backing, a Cayman trust and custody at BitGo Bank & Trust. Cashen is onboarding institutions and integration partners ahead of the planned launch. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Nomura Group / BOOSTRY approved for Canton Super Validator participation: CIP-0130 approves one participation arrangement for Nomura Group / BOOSTRY, with up to 10 reward weight in total earned through verified milestones. Read more.

CapConnect+ enters Canton vote with a plan for corporate debt settlement: CIP-0131 proposes a Super Validator role and milestones for settling commercial paper and its cash payment together on Canton. Read more.

SBI and Cambodia’s central bank plan Canton trial for tokenized government bonds: SBI and the National Bank of Cambodia have signed an MOU to test government-bond tokenization, with Canton planned as the network. The project aims to widen access to smaller investments, with interim results due at the November 15–16 Black Swan Summit. Read more.
