CANTON NEWS — WEEK 21, 2026

May 25, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of May 18–24, 2026.

ANALYSIS

Canton Network Named to BeInCrypto Institutional 100 Blockchain Infrastructure Long List: BeInCrypto included Canton Network among 15 blockchain infrastructure firms selected for its 2026 Institutional 100 “Best Blockchain Infrastructure” long list. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

Alpend Launches ONE, a Programmable Dollar Asset for Canton DeFi: Alpend has introduced ONE, a CC-backed debt instrument designed to bring dollar-targeted liquidity to Canton DeFi and expand the utility of Canton Coin as productive collateral. Read more.

SBI Group Leads Strategic Investment in Temple Digital Group: SBI Group has led a private funding round in Temple Digital Group, the top-ranked application on the Canton Network by revenue, as Temple prepares to list regulated markets and expand its institutional trading infrastructure in the second half of 2026. Read more.

Cancore Goes Live, Opening Atomic Swaps Between Canton and Public Chains: Cancore is now live for everyone, giving users a non-custodial way to swap assets between Canton Network and public blockchains through peer-to-peer atomic settlement. Read more.

Canton Network Welcomes 32 New Validator Operators: Canton Network has welcomed 32 new validator operators, including CME Group and Kakao Bank, as the network's infrastructure base continues to grow. Read more.

MARKETS

Bybit Adds USDCx Deposits on Canton Network: Bybit has enabled USDCx deposits via Canton Network, with withdrawal support to follow once liquidity thresholds are reached. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

Certora Wins Canton Foundation Grant for Daml Analysis Tooling: Certora has received a Canton Development Fund grant to build open-source tooling that helps developers and institutions analyze cross-package interactions in Daml applications before deployment. Read more.

Canton Network Approves CIP-0116, Introducing CC Locking Requirements for Featured App Status: Canton Network has approved CIP-0116, requiring Featured App applicants to lock between 5 million and 25 million CC per PartyId to qualify for and maintain Featured App designation, replacing a Foundation-led process with on-chain, market-based criteria. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Digital Asset Appoints OCC Strategy Head Oberon Knapp as Global Head of Equities: Digital Asset has named Oberon Knapp, formerly head of strategy at the Options Clearing Corporation, as its global head of equities, tasking him with leading institutional equity and derivatives market structure strategy on Canton Network. Read more.

Grayscale Names Canton Among Blockchains Poised to Benefit From Regulatory Clarity: Grayscale’s latest Stack article puts Canton alongside Ethereum, Solana and BNB Chain as networks positioned to benefit if US digital asset rules become clearer. For Canton, the signal is especially important: its strongest use case is not retail speculation, but regulated tokenization, privacy and institutional onchain finance. Read more.

JPMorgan's Kinexys Crosses $1.5 Trillion in Cumulative Volume: JPMorgan's Kinexys blockchain has crossed $1.5 trillion in cumulative volume, while the bank pursues a tokenized Treasury fund and plans to bring JPM Coin to Canton Network for institutional deposit token settlements in 2026. Read more.
