Canton Network news for the week of July 27 – August 02, 2026.

INSTITUTIONS

State of the Canton Network H1 2026: Key Findings from Palladium Labs: Palladium Labs’ 51-page State of the Canton Network – H1 2026 report maps the network’s growth across infrastructure, applications and institutional deployments, including more than 490 ecosystem participants and $9 trillion in monthly volume. Read more.

Canton Strategic Holdings Elects Three New Directors as It Advances Canton Network Strategy: Canton Strategic Holdings has elected Sean Galvin, Pamela L. Carter, and Rishi Nangalia to its Board of Directors, strengthening governance as the NASDAQ-listed company continues to build its Canton Coin-focused treasury and operating strategy. Read more.

SBI Rebrands Subsidiary to Build Financial Infrastructure on Canton Network: SBI Holdings has renamed SBI Security Solutions as SBI Digital Practice and reorganised the wholly owned subsidiary around Canton Network. The company will develop financial infrastructure and support institutional implementation in Japan and overseas. Read more.

Arqitech Deploys Canton Token Standard V2 for Institutional Cross-Chain Swaps: Arqitech has completed live institutional atomic swaps using Canton Token Standard V2, linking Canton Coin with assets on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and TRON without bridges or third-party custody. Read more.

Digital Asset Appoints Sylvain Bigaud as Senior Director of Global Business Development: Digital Asset has hired Sylvain Bigaud, formerly Regional Head of Middle East, Africa, and Turkey at Euroclear, as Senior Director of Global Business Development and Strategy, based in Dubai. Read more.

The Biggest In-Person Canton Network Hackathon of 2026 Comes to London: Cantor8 will bring university teams, developers and founders together on August 29 for a full day of building with Daml. Read more.

Chainalysis Explains Canton’s Privacy Model for Institutional Compliance: Chainalysis has identified Canton as the main example of sub-transaction privacy, where data is shared only with relevant parties while authorised stakeholders can receive access when needed. Read more.

Temple Lists Ember’s Physically Backed Gold and Silver Tokens on Canton: Temple Digital Group has listed Ember Protocol’s eXAU and eXAG for spot trading on Canton. Each token represents one troy ounce of fully allocated LBMA Good Delivery bullion held in professional vault custody. Read more.

Canton Network July 2026 Recap: Live Market Activity, Network Growth and New Products: July brought live institutional transactions, new investment, stronger network figures, product launches and wider access to Canton Coin across the growing global Canton Network ecosystem. Read more.
