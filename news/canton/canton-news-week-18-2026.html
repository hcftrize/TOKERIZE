Canton Network news for the week of April 27 – May 03, 2026.

MARKETS

Canton Tops Chain Revenue Rankings, Ahead of Tron and Ethereum: DefiLlama data showed Canton in first place on chain revenue, with Tron second and Ethereum third in the latest 24-hour rankings. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

Tokenized Treasurys Top $10.8B as Institutional Interest Grows: Tokenized Treasurys are on-chain representations of U.S. government debt instruments, classified as real-world assets, or RWAs. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Visa Adds Canton Network to Stablecoin Settlement Pilot as Run Rate Hits $7B: Visa added Canton Network to its global stablecoin settlement pilot as the program expanded to nine blockchains and reached a $7B annualized settlement run rate, up 50% from the previous quarter. Read more.

REGULATION

Global Settlement Network Brings GSX ID Compliance Layer to Canton as Validator: Global Settlement Network has been approved as a Canton validator and Featured Application, deploying GSX ID to help institutions reuse compliance credentials across tokenized capital market workflows. Read more.

ANALYSIS

Grayscale Says Canton Could Lead Early Institutional Phase of Tokenization: Grayscale Research names Canton alongside Ethereum, Solana, Avalanche, BNB Chain and Chainlink as networks positioned to benefit from the shift of capital markets onchain. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

A Look Inside the Applications Building on Canton Network: Canton Network is best known for institutional finance, but a growing app layer is forming around Canton Coin, spanning wallets, explorers, DeFi apps, payment tools, NFT products, analytics platforms and trading venues. Read more.

OneSwap Publishes Mainnet Review Showing Full Reserve Alignment: OneSwap says its active mainnet pools were fully aligned with the live ledger during a new operational review prepared for liquidity providers and institutional counterparties. Read more.
