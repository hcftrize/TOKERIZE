CANTON NEWS — WEEK 30, 2026

July 27, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of July 20–26, 2026.

TECHNOLOGY

Canton Network Has Burned 4.3 Billion Canton Coin Since Launch: Canton Network has burned 4.3 billion CC since mid-2024, worth more than $500 million, with daily burns accelerating and a CIP draft expected in September that would extend burning to every synchronizer on the network. Read more.

Ekiden Joins Canton Network as a Featured App: Ekiden has been approved as a Featured App on the Canton Network. The project is building institutional derivatives infrastructure on Canton, with a hybrid RFQ and CLOB model for efficient on-chain market execution. Read more.

OneSwap v2 Is Live on Canton: OneSwap v2 is now live with a rebuilt swap engine, single-transaction swaps, a built-in platform wallet, and prepaid gas support for lower network fees. Read more.

MARKETS

Premia Acquires Raven Market in All-Cash Deal, Absorbing Canton-Native Derivatives Infrastructure: Premia has acquired Raven Market in an all-cash deal, integrating the Canton-native institutional derivatives platform into its offering for 38 institutional clients managing more than $127 million in assets under management. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Digital Asset Adds Shinhan and Standard Chartered to Funding Round: Digital Asset has added Shinhan Financial Group and SC Ventures by Standard Chartered to its previously announced oversubscribed funding round, expanding its institutional investor base. Read more.

From DTCC to Corporate Treasury: Yuval Rooz Outlines Canton’s Next Phase: Digital Asset co-founder and CEO Yuval Rooz discusses DTCC’s first production activity, Canton mainnet extensions, network burn economics and a corporate treasury use case that could bring hundreds or thousands of businesses on-chain. Read more.

Hydra X Launches HX Gateway API to Speed Canton Deployments: Hydra X has launched the HX Gateway API, a REST layer for institutions and developers building on Canton without specialised Daml expertise. The company says it can speed go-to-market by up to 80% and cut deployment time by 50%. Read more.

Canton Strategic Holdings Sells Gravitas to Focus on Canton Network: Canton Strategic Holdings has sold its biotech R&D arm, Gravitas Life Sciences, to Gravitas Collective Corp. The board-approved deal closed on July 17 as the Nasdaq-listed company narrows its operating strategy around the Canton Network. Read more.

Nasdaq Pilots Tokenized Collateral on Canton Network as Q2 Earnings Hit Record Highs: Nasdaq disclosed a successful Canton Network tokenized collateral pilot through its Calypso platform during a record Q2 2026 earnings call, as the exchange operator reported $1.5 billion in net revenue, $1 trillion in index AUM, and the largest IPO in history. Read more.

Digital Asset Raises Additional $10 Million From Shinhan Financial Group and SC Ventures, Bringing Round to $365 Million: Digital Asset has raised an additional $10 million from Shinhan Financial Group and SC Ventures, extending its funding round to $365 million at a $2 billion valuation.
