CANTON NEWS — WEEK 36, 2026

September 07, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of August 31 – September 06, 2026.

INSTITUTIONS

Hanwha Plans Its First Tokenized Product on Canton: Hanwha Investment & Securities is preparing a Canton-based product issuance as it develops a digital-asset platform initially aimed at professional investors. Read more.

DTCC Tokenization Service Set for October Commercial Launch After July Production Trades: DTCC's tokenization service is set for commercial launch in October 2026 following successful July production trades on Canton Network, with more than 50 institutions including BlackRock, JPMorgan, and Goldman Sachs participating in the Industry Working Group. Read more.

FinTech Magazine Names Digital Asset Among Its Top 10 Digital Asset Platforms: Digital Asset has been included in FinTech Magazine’s Top 10 Digital Asset Platforms, with Daml and Canton highlighted as central parts of the company’s work. Read more.

HashKey Joins DTCC Tokenization Working Group as First Asian Digital Asset Provider: HashKey Group has joined DTCC's tokenization working group as its first Asian digital asset provider, ahead of DTCC's planned October 2026 commercial service launch on Canton Network and Hyperledger Besu infrastructure. Read more.

Fireblocks Maps Out Institutional Collateral Workflows on Canton: Fireblocks has published a practical guide to the Canton tools it supports for institutional collateral workflows, covering custody, wallets, validator options, contract calls, transaction approvals and reporting. Read more.

TreasurySpring’s $4 Billion Canton Plan Enters Governance Vote: TreasurySpring’s Super Validator proposal has entered formal Canton voting. It begins with a $10 million Fixed Term Fund target and lays out a proposed path to $4 billion outstanding on Canton within 12 months of approval. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

arCCade confirms Canton Featured App Status as early access nears: arCCade has confirmed its Featured App status on Canton Network as it prepares to welcome its first users. The waitlist is open, with the first access codes due to be distributed soon. Read more.

Sync Insights Launches Unified Workspace for Analytics, Monitoring, and Automation on Canton Network: Sync Insights has launched a unified workspace for Canton Network combining analytics, automated workflows, governance visibility, private transfer insights, and transaction parsing, building on a platform that has delivered more than 800,000 insights since June 2025. Read more.

Canton Loop Opens TestNet Trial for New Transaction Fee Model: Canton Loop has opened limited TestNet access to a new fee model that shows users the network cost before they approve a transaction. Read more.

DSRV Brings Canton Into ITCEN Group’s Live Cafe Payment System: South Korean infrastructure provider DSRV has moved ITCEN Group’s Cafe de CEN ordering and payment service onto Canton, connecting it with the group’s ERP for real-time order, payment and settlement processing. Read more.

Canton Approves 15 Validator Operators as 25 More Enter Review: Fifteen organizations can now begin deploying Canton validator infrastructure, while a new group of 25 applicants enters community review. MainNet access still requires a separate allocation. Read more.

Canton Development Fund Pays 9.37 Million CC Across Eight Milestones: The Canton Development Fund has paid 9,368,809 CC across eight completed milestones covering application rewards, token support, wallet infrastructure, testing, developer tools and governance. Read more.

Alpend Completes Major Protocol Upgrade on Canton: Alpend has upgraded its Canton money market with multiparty hosting, distributed protocol controls and a Halborn-audited codebase. The upgrade also raises supply caps and introduces Ascent 2 rewards for migrated positions. Read more.

Five North Launches Paid Plans for Lighthouse API: Five North has introduced paid Lighthouse API plans alongside its continuing free tier. The new structure offers different levels of access for projects using Canton data. Read more.

Digital Asset Releases Canton 3.5.16 With Reliability Improvements: Canton 3.5.16 is now available with reliability fixes, stronger timestamp validation, lower memory use during one internal process and expanded API documentation. Read more.

Digital Asset CEO Confirms September CIP Draft for Network-Wide CC Burning: Digital Asset CEO Yuval Rooz has confirmed that the Canton Improvement Proposal extending CC burning and validator rewards to every synchronizer on the Canton Network remains on track for submission this month, following earlier remarks from co-founder Shaul Kfir outlining the plan. Read more.

FTP Tech Resubmits Featured App Application for Canton x402 Payments: FTP Tech has resubmitted its Featured App application for x402 tools on Canton. The service lets AI agents pay for individual online requests from their own wallets using Canton Coin or USDCx. Read more.

Hashrupt Presents Six Verity Products on Canton: Hashrupt has presented six products built around Verity, its shared platform on Canton. They cover lending, treasury, invoicing, project work and fundraising, with additional Settla and Backr features still in development. Read more.

Ones Unveils Canton Business Account and Confirms MVP Is Ready: Ones has introduced a financial platform for individuals, businesses and AI agents. Its business account is being built on Canton and is designed to bring payments, cards, payroll, invoicing, treasury and team management into one place. Read more.

Squid Intents Launches Multi-Chain Swaps for Canton: Squid Intents is now live on Canton, adding routes from supported assets on 30 chains, new liquidity for CC swaps and integration tools for wallets and applications. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

French crypto platform Coinhouse adds Canton Coin: French crypto platform Coinhouse has added Canton Coin, allowing customers to buy CC with euros, then hold, sell or exchange it within their Coinhouse accounts. Read more.

Temple Digital Group and HIFI Partner to Enable Direct Bank-to-Canton Funding and Settlement: Temple Digital Group and HIFI have partnered to enable direct bank-to-Canton funding and withdrawal, allowing Temple users to deposit US dollars, trade on Canton, and withdraw back to their bank account 24/7 through HIFI's stablecoin conversion infrastructure. Read more.

Rocky Wallet Plans Support for Hecto Assets on Canton: Rocky plans to support Hecto ecosystem assets in Rocky Wallet and make the wallet available as a connection inside Hecto products. Technical details and release timing are still to come. Read more.

Tradecraft Reports More Than $3 Million in Canton TVL: Tradecraft reports that the total value locked across its Canton liquidity pools has passed $3 million. The figure measures assets held in the pools that support trading on the platform. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

eesee Outlines Plans for a Connected Financial Hub on Canton: eesee has outlined plans to connect its launchpad with portfolio tracking and vaults on Canton. The company reports 91 launches and more than $10.9 million raised through its existing launchpad. Read more.

South Korea Sets Roadmap for Tokenized Stocks, Bonds and Funds: South Korea will begin the first phase of its tokenized securities roadmap in February 2027. The plan starts with institutional funds and bonds, unlisted shares and fractional investments, with later phases planned for public securities and onchain settlement. Read more.
