CANTON NEWS — WEEK 38, 2026

September 21, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of September 14–20, 2026, covering INSTITUTIONS, TECHNOLOGY, ECOSYSTEM, MARKETS, TOKENIZATION.

INSTITUTIONS

Tokenovate Settles Intraday Repo on Canton Network Using USDC and Common Domain Model: Tokenovate has completed an intraday repo on Canton Network using USDC and the FINOS Common Domain Model, demonstrating programmable, privacy-preserving post-trade settlement on institutional blockchain infrastructure. Read more.

S&P Global Leads $110 Million Series B for Kaiko as Canton Foundation Joins Strategic Working Group: S&P Global has led a $110 million Series B expansion for Kaiko, with the Canton Foundation joining a new Strategic Industry Working Group on tokenised market data infrastructure, as Kaiko deepens its existing work with Canton Network on the iBoxx Treasury index and CC pricing benchmarks. Read more.

Digital Asset named in UK tokenisation feedback ahead of 2026 roadmap: Digital Asset Holdings (Canton) is listed among respondents to the UK’s consultation on tokenisation in wholesale markets. The FCA and Bank of England will use the feedback to develop a joint roadmap later this year, with collateral movement a leading priority. Read more.

Edel joins DTC’s digital assets working group: Edel has joined the industry working group helping shape the DTCC Tokenization Service. The Canton builder will contribute feedback alongside major financial institutions ahead of the service’s planned launch later this year. Read more.

Cashen Joins Canton Foundation Collateral Subcommittee Alongside DTCC, BNY, and Euroclear: Cashen, Canton Network's CC locking marketplace, has joined the Canton Foundation's Collateral Subcommittee chaired by DTCC, contributing to the Cross-Application Collateral Mobility workstream alongside BNY, Euroclear, and Broadridge. Read more.

Japan's FSA Names On-Chain Finance a 2026 Policy Priority as MUFG Tests JGB Repos on Canton Network: Japan's FSA has named on-chain finance its top 2026 policy priority, with MUFG's JGB repo testing on Canton Network already selected for the FSA's Payment Innovation Project as the regulator builds out the legal, institutional, and collaborative infrastructure for blockchain-based financial markets. Read more.

$EDEL is Finally Taking Its Rightful Seat at the Table: Edel Finance is deepening its role in institutional tokenization through its involvement in the DTC Digital Assets Solutions Industry Working Group and its growing presence in the Canton ecosystem. As traditional assets move onchain, $EDEL is positioning itself around the infrastructure, liquidity, collateral, and settlement systems that could shape the next phase of tokenized finance. Read more.

South Korean Asset Managers Build Digital Asset Infrastructure as Domestic Regulations Lag Overseas Markets: South Korean asset managers are launching digital asset ETFs abroad and building blockchain partnerships at home, including Shinhan Asset Management's agreement with the Canton Foundation, as they prepare for an expected opening of the domestic digital asset product market. Read more.

Kyobo Life and SBI Complete Cross-Border Yen-Won Stablecoin Test on Canton Network: Kyobo Life and SBI have completed a Canton Network pilot testing direct yen-won stablecoin exchange without a dollar intermediate leg, modelling cross-border institutional settlement in what Kyobo Life describes as South Korea's first such test by an insurer. Read more.

TreasurySpring Approved as Canton Network Super Validator to Record Fixed-Term Fund Share Ownership On-Chain: TreasurySpring has been approved as a Canton Network Super Validator, with the first milestone focused on recording Fixed-Term Fund Share ownership on Canton's ledger as the authoritative ownership register, with no change to the existing client experience. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

Utila highlights Canton in new blockchain privacy guide for banks: Utila’s new guide explores how banks can use blockchains while keeping sensitive financial information private. Canton features alongside Aleo and Zama, with a focus on controlling who can see transaction details. Read more.

Intercepta Integrates Compliance Engine With Canton Network for Real-Time Sanctions Screening and AML Checks: Intercepta has integrated its Compliance Engine with Canton Network, enabling real-time sanctions screening, AML checks, and fund flow traceability through designated observer roles within Canton's privacy-preserving architecture, without altering the network's underlying privacy model. Read more.

LYNC brings one-command deployment to Canton developers: LYNC has introduced canton-deploy, a tool that combines several steps needed to deploy an application’s Daml packages and set up users. Developers can also check deployments, inspect contracts and manage network configurations from the same tool. Read more.

Canton approves reward change for batched asset settlements: An approved change removes a cap that disadvantaged applications settling several trades together. The broader reward framework remains under review. Read more.

Canton 3.5.18 adds reliability fixes and a security update: The September 17 release improves catch-up, corrects misleading warnings and includes a security fix for node operators. Read more.

Loop brings prepaid network fees to Canton MainNet: Loop's Fee Balance is live on Canton MainNet, giving users a separate prepaid balance for transaction costs. The rollout also brings an update for applications that submit transactions from their own servers. Read more.

Cancore brings AI-assisted trading to Canton: Cancore MCP lets users turn a plain-language request into a proposed trade. The wallet supplies the live quote, and the user reviews and signs before any funds move. Read more.

Console adds individual balances for Canton network fees: Console users can now fund their own Canton traffic fees with CC or top up another participant's balance. The feature gives each account a separate way to cover its network usage. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

P2P Company seeks Featured App status for Canton escrow trading: P2P Company has applied for Featured App status for its live Canton escrow service. The platform supports peer-to-peer trades of CC, CBTC and cETH, with buyers paying sellers directly in stablecoins or local currency. Read more.

Temple Digital Group Launches Virtual Orderbooks for Custom Trading Venues on Canton Network: Temple Digital Group has opened up its Lightspeed trading engine to select institutions through Virtual Orderbooks, a primitive that lets operators deploy private, compliant spot, prediction, and perpetual markets on Canton Network. Read more.

Canton Network Receives 21 New Validator Applications Spanning Finance, Infrastructure, and Emerging Market Use Cases: Twenty-one organisations have submitted Canton Network validator applications ahead of a September 23 deadline, including interest rate swap infrastructure, undercollateralised credit markets, trade finance, yield routing, tokenised gold, and a broad range of Southeast Asian and emerging market builders. Read more.

Alpend and BitSafe add CC rewards for CBTC lenders: Alpend has partnered with BitSafe to reward CBTC deposits with Canton Coin on top of the interest earned from lending. Deposits remain subject to the pool's available capacity. Read more.

Ekiden passes $1 million in trading as early users get lower fees: Ekiden's invite-only MainNet has passed $1 million in trading volume across 15,978 trades. A temporary fee offer and new leverage limits accompany the milestone. Read more.

MARKETS

CC/USDCx Pair Goes Live on Kora's Permissionless Orderbook: CC/USDCx is now live for trading on Kora's permissionless orderbook, bringing together Canton Coin and Circle's USDCx stablecoin on Canton Network, with the pair available via xReserve. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

Kora adds USDCx trading pairs for gold and silver on Canton: Kora users can now trade eXAU and eXAG against USDCx. The new gold and silver pairs follow a funding option that lets users move USDC from Ethereum directly into a Kora account. Read more.
