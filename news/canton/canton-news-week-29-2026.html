CANTON NEWS — WEEK 29, 2026

July 20, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of July 13–19, 2026.

INSTITUTIONS

Grayscale Names Canton Among Five Networks Positioned for Tokenized Equities: Grayscale says Canton Network is positioned to benefit as equity tokenization moves into regulated market infrastructure. Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain and Avalanche could capture other parts of the market as three ownership models develop. Read more.

Digital Asset / Canton Joins 54-Firm UK Wholesale Tokenisation Taskforce: Digital Asset / Canton is among 54 firms supporting a UK programme to move wholesale-market tokenisation beyond pilots and into live markets. The taskforce will begin with tokenised repo and establish nine Action Groups over the next 12 months. Read more.

DTCC Says DTC-Held Asset Conversions Occurred on Canton and Hyperledger Besu: DTCC says assets held at DTC were converted into tokens used in real production trades. The digital conversions occurred on Canton, a public network, and Hyperledger Besu, DTCC’s private network. Read more.

Hanwha Invests KRW 30 Billion in Digital Asset Holdings, Plans Canton Network Participation: Hanwha Investment & Securities has completed an approximately KRW 30 billion strategic investment in Digital Asset Holdings LLC and plans to participate in the Canton Network ecosystem. Read more.

Canton Strategic Releases Q2 2026 Canton Network Ecosystem Report: Canton Strategic Holdings released its second-quarter Canton Network ecosystem report, covering governance developments, institutional adoption and application-layer expansion. A public webinar will be held on July 22. Read more.

Canton Network Q2 2026: A Deep Dive Into Transactions, Apps and Validators: Canton Strategic Holdings' Q2 report says Canton Network averaged 2.28 million daily transactions, Featured Apps increased from 47 to 107, and approximately 200 new Validators launched. This deep dive examines the reported activity, institutional transactions, application growth, governance changes and data qualifications behind those figures. Read more.

Visa Stablecoin Platform Launch Adds Weight to Canton Network's Institutional Momentum: Visa has launched the Visa Stablecoin Platform for institutional stablecoin issuance and management, extending a year of stablecoin infrastructure development that includes becoming a Canton Network Super Validator and testing private stablecoin settlement on the network. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

Edel Finance Prepares Canton-Native Perpetuals Venue for Tokenized Assets: Edel Markets is expected to launch later this quarter as a Canton-native perpetuals venue for tokenized equities, commodities and future multi-asset markets. Its design combines private positions, order-book execution, API access and onchain settlement. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

Temple Digital Group and BitGo Partner to Bring Qualified Custody to Tokenized Assets on Canton Network: Temple Digital Group and BitGo have partnered to bring regulated custody through BitGo Bank and Trust to tokenized assets on Canton Network, giving institutional clients access to capital markets through Temple alongside a qualified custody solution they can trust. Read more.

Canton Network Powers Part of DTCC's Multi-Chain Tokenization Push, Balchunas Says: Bloomberg ETF analyst Eric Balchunas confirmed the Canton public chain is being used in DTCC's latest tokenization project, alongside Hyperledger Besu, as part of a multi-chain strategy involving more than 40 firms including BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

Temple Digital Group Executes Onchain US Treasury Repo Trade on Canton Network via DTCC Tokenization Service: Temple Digital Group participated in one of the first onchain US Treasury repo trades on Canton Network through DTCC's Tokenization Service, settling a tokenized Treasury against Circle's USDCx with atomic delivery-versus-payment, ahead of the service's expected October 2026 launch. Read more.

Cashen Passes 50 Platforms as Canton Network's Credit Marketplace Gains Traction: Cashen, the bilateral CC delegation marketplace backed by Cumberland DRW company Ergonia, has passed 50 platforms onboarded, cementing its position as one of the fastest growing startups on Canton Network by dollar value and the leading destination for locking CC under CIP-0116. Read more.

Agentic Trading Infrastructure on Canton Network: Silvana is an agent interaction layer for tokenized assets on Canton Network, providing an orderbook, developer tools, and infrastructure for automated trading workflows. The platform supports four trading pairs, more than 100 deployed agents, and over 3,000 onboarded traders, alongside an ongoing campaign with Edel Finance. Read more.
