CANTON NEWS — WEEK 32, 2026

August 10, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of August 03–09, 2026.

MARKETS

Bitrue to List Canton’s $CC Token for Spot Trading: Bitrue will list Canton’s $CC token, with CC/USDT spot trading opening on August 4 and withdrawals becoming available the following day. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Franklin Templeton Becomes a Canton Super Validator: Franklin Templeton Digital Assets has announced its new role as a Super Validator on Canton Network, expanding its involvement from tokenized fund deployment into network infrastructure. Its potential Super Validator weight is tied to milestones covering treasury activity, 24/7 fund operations and production volume. Read more.

FOMO Pay Receives Canton Validator Approval: FOMO Pay has received validator approval on Canton and plans to use the network for institutional B2B payments and settlement. Read more.

BOOSTRY Plans Canton Multi-Chain Wallet With Digital Asset: BOOSTRY and Digital Asset are working together on a corporate multi-chain wallet scheduled for launch by the end of 2026, beginning with support for Canton. Read more.

1exchange Approved as Canton Validator for RWA Tokenization: 1exchange has been approved as a Canton validator and plans to use the network’s privacy-preserving infrastructure for future RWA tokenization, listings and trading. Read more.

Nano Labs Approved as Canton Network Validator: Nano Labs has been approved to operate a validator node on Canton, joining the network’s global validator ecosystem. Read more.

ISLA Joins Canton Foundation as Associate Member to Shape Digital Securities Finance Standards: ISLA has joined the Canton Foundation as an associate member, placing the securities finance industry's leading trade body inside Canton Network's collateral and technology working groups. Read more.

SBI Digital Practice and Nodeinfra Plan Japan-Korea Stablecoin Payment Network: SBI Digital Practice and Nodeinfra plan to develop the Musubi Project, a cross-border payment and settlement network built on Canton, beginning with yen and won test tokens. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

Temple Digital Group and RedStone Partner to Bring Pricing Infrastructure to Commodity Markets on Canton: Temple Digital Group and RedStone have partnered to bring RedStone's institutional price feeds to Temple's newly launched gold and silver commodity markets on Canton Network. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

PartyLayer Passes 106,000 npm Downloads and Announces Its New Roadmap: The open source wallet connection layer for the Canton Network crosses a six figure adoption milestone and sets out its plan for the next twelve months. Read more.

Zenith Demonstrates Canton-EVM Atomic Swap on Test Network: Zenith has demonstrated an atomic swap between a CIP-56 asset on Canton and an ERC-20 asset on Zenith as work continues toward EVM integration with Canton MainNet. Read more.
