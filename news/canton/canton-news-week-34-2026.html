CANTON NEWS — WEEK 34, 2026

August 24, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of August 17–23, 2026, covering MARKETS, ECOSYSTEM, INSTITUTIONS, TECHNOLOGY.

MARKETS

Canton Coin Lists on SolCex: SolCex, the first centralized exchange launched on Solana, has listed Canton Coin (CC), giving traders a new venue to trade CC on the platform. Read more.

Interstice Digital Launches Cross-Chain Swap Engine Connecting Canton Network to Robinhood Chain, Solana, and Ethereum: Interstice Digital and FalconX have launched a non-custodial cross-chain swap engine connecting Canton Network to Robinhood Chain, Solana, and Ethereum, named a Featured App on Canton as institutional and retail liquidity paths converge on the network. Read more.

Canton Coin Now Available on Ledger, Bringing Institutional-Grade Asset to Retail Hardware Wallets: Canton Coin is now available natively on Ledger hardware wallets, opening CC self-custody to individual holders for the first time as the token moves beyond its institutional and validator roots. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

ChainSafe Launches x402 Facilitator for Native Data Monetization on Canton Network: ChainSafe has released the x402 Facilitator, open source infrastructure that brings account-free HTTP 402 micro-billing to Canton Network, enabling autonomous AI agents to discover, query, and pay for enterprise data in real time. Read more.

Canton Coin Goes Live on Bitstamp by Robinhood With USD and EUR Markets: Canton Coin is now available on Bitstamp by Robinhood, with CC/USD and CC/EUR spot markets open for trading. Bitstamp’s public data also shows Canton Network deposits and withdrawals are enabled, subject to regional access. Read more.

Canton Clarifies Featured App Rewards for Multi-Asset Transactions: Canton’s Tokenomics Committee has clarified how Featured App reward weight is calculated when a transaction includes more than one Featured Asset. Ordinary transactions share the available weight across those assets, while approved delivery-versus-payment venues use a separate calculation when both sides of an exchange settle together. Read more.

Canton Network Welcomes 10 New Validator Applicants Spanning Finance, Agriculture, AI, and Retail: The Canton Foundation has published its latest batch of validator operator submissions, covering 10 applicants ranging from AI agent payment platforms to RWA issuers and luxury retail, with a one-week window for concerns before implicit approval on August 26, 2026. Read more.

R1M Seeks Canton Featured App Status for Sentinel: R1M has applied for Canton Featured App status for R1M Sentinel, its validator-monitoring service. The team plans to introduce premium subscriptions paid in Canton Coin in early September. Read more.

INSTITUTIONS

Temple Trading Data Goes Live on Token Terminal: Temple Digital Group’s market data is now available across Token Terminal, giving users access to trading activity from the Canton-based platform. Read more.

CAC Launches Pactora With Canton Network Support: Japanese technology company CAC has launched Pactora, a multichain platform for issuing, managing and settling tokenized assets across Canton Network, Avalanche and Solana. Read more.

Canton Approves 17 Validator Operators and Issues New MainNet Allocations: Canton’s latest governance updates approve 17 validator operators, while a separate MainNet allocation notice expands access across 22 organizations and 26 allocation entries. Read more.

Digital Asset and American Idea Foundation Pilot Blockchain-Based Benefit Distribution on Canton Network: Digital Asset and the American Idea Foundation have announced a collaboration to pilot blockchain-based government benefit distribution using Canton Network, testing a unified, programmable system for delivering state RISE benefits to families across multiple assistance programs. Read more.

HSBC CST Receives Featured App Status on Canton TestNet: HSBC CST has received Featured App status on Canton Network’s TestNet after all 13 Super Validators supported the proposal. The right became active on August 21, adding another milestone to HSBC’s work across the network. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

Canton Enables CIP-0104 Models and Featured App Weight Voting: Canton has enabled CIP-0104’s supporting Daml models and Featured App weight-voting interface on MainNet, advancing the network’s move toward traffic-based app rewards. Read more.

Canton Foundation Launches Super Validator Operations Calendar: The Canton Foundation has launched a web-based master calendar for Super Validator operations, giving operators and app providers a clearer view of upgrades, dependencies and planned network events. Read more.

Canton Development Fund Allocates More Than 162 Million CC Across 32 Proposals: Canton’s Development Fund has allocated more than 162 million CC across 32 approved proposals supporting protocol development, infrastructure, security and ecosystem tools. Funding is released as teams complete and pass their agreed milestones. Read more.
