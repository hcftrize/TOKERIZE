CANTON NEWS — WEEK 47, 2026

November 23, 2026 | Tokerize Editorial

Canton Network news for the week of November 16–22, 2026.

INSTITUTIONS

Nomura, NRI and BOOSTRY plan Canton links for financial firms: NRI is developing a connection to existing securities systems. BOOSTRY says its Canton-focused institutional wallet entered production in September. Read more.

Canton Strategic doubles quarterly revenue to $3.05 million: Validation and locking services drove Q3 growth and the first positive adjusted EBITDA. A $54.65 million unrealized loss on CC holdings contributed to a $35.08 million net loss. Read more.

The Evolution of Canton Data Infrastructure: An examination of how Canton's data ecosystem has developed, the capabilities supporting its financial markets, and the emerging opportunity for specialised financial intelligence and credit and collateral benchmarks. Read more.

Talos, Marex and Broadridge plan tokenized Treasury financing on Canton: The planned workflow connects trading, prime brokerage and settlement for DTC-tokenized securities, beginning with US Treasuries and financing beyond traditional market hours. Read more.

Progmat takes its Canton bond sandbox plan to Japan’s finance ministry study group: Progmat’s November 20 presentation sets out how participants could test bond and cash transfers together, with Canton Testnet connecting to repo and collateral platforms. Read more.

TECHNOLOGY

PartyLayer Passes 200,000 npm Downloads as Canton Developer Toolkit Expands: The open-source toolkit from Cayvox Labs brings wallet connectivity, interfaces in 14 languages, application authentication tools and reusable transfer components to teams building on Canton. Read more.

ChainSafe provides Canton DevNet infrastructure for OpenZeppelin: ChainSafe is running the DevNet endpoints OpenZeppelin uses to upload Daml packages and test its Canton contracts. The arrangement supports development of its library and reference applications. Read more.

Palladium Labs open-sources Chainlink oracle kit for Canton developers: canton-oracle-kit brings together signed-price verification, freshness checks and an example updater. Palladium says the underlying oracle path is used by Alpend on MainNet, with operator permissions central to the design. Read more.

Canton issues Java4Shell mitigation guidance for node operators: The Canton team recommends disabling Java’s Base64 intrinsic while operators check their Java distribution and packaged components for patches. Vendor fixes are already available for affected builds. Read more.

Digital Asset outlines covalidation adoption across Canton wallets and apps: Digital Asset names Loop, T-RIZE and Bron as adopters of multi-hosted infrastructure. The model distributes transaction confirmations across chosen operators, reducing dependence on a single host. Read more.

Stratalink’s PoLi Oracle records liquidity scores on Canton MainNet: A new case study details a service operating since October, with signed liquidity assessments for BTC, ETH, SOL and CC across two venues. Access from external consumers’ own validators is planned for a later phase. Read more.

Diviko joins Canton Foundation with focus on self-custody and interoperability: The Swiss developer brings experience in wallets, shared approvals and cross-chain infrastructure. The Foundation’s November 20 spotlight follows its earlier membership announcement. Read more.

Canton Development Fund approves 14.3 million CC for locking rules and developer tools: Digital Asset and Obsidian will implement Canton’s coin-locking rules. A separate BitDynamics grant reserves 51% of its budget for adoption by external projects. Read more.

ECOSYSTEM

Canton Roundup: wallet upgrades, developer funding and community campaigns: Canton releases new wallet tools, four teams seek development funding, and Canborsa and Cauri open community campaigns. Plus trading figures, cumulative burn and The Hub’s token warning. Read more.

Alpend introduces Adula as a separate home for ONE: Palladium Labs is separating the identities of its lending protocol and ONE issuance protocol. Alpend says the underlying mechanics are unchanged; public collateral minting remains listed as coming soon. Read more.

Canton Roundup: paymaster vote, Supanova market data and validator updates: CIP-0132 enters voting, Grofty Lens adds Supanova markets, and maintainers test a recovery procedure for affected validators. Plus Splice releases, DPM Trace testing, NFT-profile sponsorship and a Seoul developer meetup. Read more.

Canton approves CapConnect+ Super Validator plan with weight tied to milestones: CIP-0131 passed with 12 votes in favour and one non-vote. CapConnect+ can earn up to five units of reward weight as it brings commercial-paper settlement and institutional participants onto Canton. Read more.

Canton Roundup: reward timetable, lower fees and developer funding: The latest updates include the TBAR MainNet dry-run schedule, OnePump fees, Canborsa markets, a 6.2 million CC fiat-connector request, Daml tooling and operator notices. Read more.

Supanova introduces V2 with mobile trading and CC funding routes: Supanova’s V2 announcement highlights bridging into Canton Coin, trading from a phone and creating tokens. Its current launchpad guide sets out the funding steps, fees and graduation rules. Read more.

Kiwoom, CSDBR and Ironlight feature in Canton validator application batch: Seven submissions enter the objection period ending November 25. The applications describe institutional testing, Brazilian market infrastructure and tokenized-securities settlement. Read more.

TOKENIZATION

Fairmint plans Canton venue for tokenized shares with Tradecraft as first AMM: Fairmint TSV would connect liquidity pools to issuer-sponsored shares, starting on Canton. The planned venue is separate from the existing TSV LLC market and has not begun trading. Read more.

MARKETS

aSOL begins trading on Cantex and OneSwap: The SOL-backed token reached two Canton trading venues on consecutive days. Cantex says qualified participants can redeem aSOL for SOL. Read more.

Broadridge DLR crosses $120 trillion in cumulative repo par value: The November 20 data show $442.72 billion in daily par value, $405.04 billion in turnover and 9,854 trades. It was the largest daily par value in the published series since April 1. Read more.
